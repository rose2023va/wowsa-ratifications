## Notes

- **Attachments.** Emails 1, 2, 3, 6, 8 and 13 carry one attachment, generally the observer log.
- **Email 16** is the only one whose subject does not carry the Swim ID.
- **Email 8** has no CC available, so Quinn appears as a full To recipient alongside the swimmer
  and observer.
- **Email 14** is composed manually. Its per swim status lines, "2 reviews received, needs 1 more",
  are counted by a person.
- **Emails 4, 6, 8, 11 and 17** each contain a copy defect. See [12-defects.md](12-defects.md).
- The **appeal process**, thirty days to submit additional documentation, appears only in email 18.
