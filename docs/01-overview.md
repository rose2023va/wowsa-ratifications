# Overview

## What ratification is

WOWSA ratifies independent marathon swims in bodies of water not served by a local or regional
governing body. Ratification confirms that a swim was completed under marathon swimming standards.
It is a prerequisite for record consideration, not a record in itself.

Only unassisted marathon swims are eligible for records. Assisted swims receive certification
rather than ratification and are not record eligible.

**Route approval is not ratification.** Approval of a route means the plan is sound, nothing more.
Swimmers conflate the two constantly.

**The ratified distance is the straight-line tangent** between start and finish, not the distance
actually swum.

See [reference/glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) for the full vocabulary.

## Volume

Roughly ten ratifications a month at peak. Around a hundred a year.

## Who is involved

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Applicant | The swimmer, or someone buying on their behalf | Buys the product, submits the planning form, submits a post-swim narrative |
| Independent observer | Named by the swimmer | Signs an agreement, observes and logs the swim, submits the post-swim report |
| Ratification Committee | A pool of twelve | Three of them review each swim and give a graded assessment |
| Admin | Rose | Executes the entire process. Measures routes, decides when a swim has happened, builds every page, chases every gap, records every outcome |
| Director | Quinn | Decides route approvals. Handles social posting |

The buyer and the swimmer are held as separate people throughout. Table 1 in Airtable carries
billing name and email separately from the swimmer's name and email.

## What the process produces

For a ratified swim:

- A public ratification page on the ratifications WordPress site
- A certificate and social assets
- An entry in WOWSA's institutional record

For a swim that is not ratified:

- A published page recording the determination
- A certificate and social assets worded as a **verified attempt**
- A 30 day window to appeal

## The three evidence criteria

Stated identically to swimmers, to observers, and on the committee form:

1. Observer logs and observer report
2. Photo and video evidence with accurate timestamps and geotags
3. GPS tracks, raw exports preferred

Photo metadata timestamps are the source of truth for when a photograph was taken.
