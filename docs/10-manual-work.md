## Where the leverage is

Ordered by manual time removed per unit of work to change it.

1. **Detecting that a swim has happened.** The swim date is already captured on the planning
   form. Nothing needs to be gathered. This unblocks the swimmer, removes a recurring check, and
   is the direct cause of the largest invisible delay in the process. See
   [11-constraints.md](11-constraints.md).
2. **Page assembly.** The single largest recurring cost. The page is a render of a record, and
   most of its content already exists in structured form.
3. **Certificates and social assets.** Around thirty per month, produced from templates, with
   details typed in that already exist in the forms.
4. **Observer log transcription.** The observer already produced this log. It is being retyped.
5. **Reviewer counting and chasing.** Currently done by opening each form and counting.
